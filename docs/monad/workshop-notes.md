# Workshop notes

To-dos from the Monad Metropolis livestreams. Sponsors Veil targets for bounties (see `tracks-eligibility.md`) are marked **(bounty)**.

## Product and pitch

From the Monad, Chainstack and Kuru workshops. These apply to any project.

- Who is the ideal customer, and why might they still not use Veil? (Chainstack)
- Who are the strongest competitors, and why are they strong? (Chainstack)
- Ask users on Reddit (use the Ask AI button), Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, TikTok and others. Aim for 100. (Monad)
- Focus on smooth UX, especially for people outside crypto Twitter. (Kuru)
- Check the judges' profiles to see what kinds of projects they like. (Kuru)
- Add a sound to the UI. (Monad)
- Get the slides from Pareen. (Monad)
